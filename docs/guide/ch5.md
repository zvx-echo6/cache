# 5. Notebooks

## What notebooks are

"Notebooks" is where you write things down on your phone. You keep several notebooks. Each is made from a template:
- "Journal": dated entries about what happened.
- "Log": something you do again and again, such as a generator test.
- "Notes": plans, checklists and lists you keep updating.
- "Places": places with notes and photos, shown on the map.

![The Notebooks list](shot:notebooks)

A fresh install has a Journal.

Open "Notebooks" from "Modules". It lists your notebooks and how many entries each has. "Log again" shows the logs you used last. "Search" looks across all notebooks.

## Make a notebook

Make a new notebook from a template.

![New notebook](shot:notebook-new)

1. [Open "Notebooks"](cache://notebooks) and tap "New notebook".
2. Under "Start from", choose "Journal", "Log", "Notes" or "Places". Or pick a ready-made log under "Starter logs".
3. Type a name.
4. Tap "Create".

- If you see "You already have a notebook with this name.", choose another name.

## Edit, delete or share a notebook

1. Open the notebook.
2. Tap "Edit notebook" to change its name, or turn "Show on map" or "Private" on or off.
3. Tap "Delete notebook" to remove it. A pop-up says how many entries go with it. This can't be undone.
4. Tap "Add to Home" to put it on "Home".

![Edit notebook](shot:notebook-edit)

## Journal

A Journal keeps dated entries: what happened, where, and what you did.

![A Journal entry](shot:notebook-entry)

1. Open the Journal and tap "New entry". When it's empty the button says "Write an entry".
2. Optional: type a "Title (optional)".
3. Write your text. It formats as you type. See Write with Markdown.
4. Optional: tap "Add photo".
5. Tap "Done".

Cache saves as you type. Each entry has a time you can change, and a place. The place starts as where you are.

With "Show on map" on, the entries appear on the map.

- If you leave before "Done", the journal shows "Unfinished entry · Tue 4:12 PM" (the time varies) with "Continue".
- If there's no position yet, the entry says "No position yet" and still saves.
- If you see "Not saved. Your phone may be out of space.", free space and tap "Try again". Your text is kept.

To delete an entry, open it and choose "Delete". Tap "Undo" on "Entry deleted" to bring it back.

## Log notebooks

A Log is for something you do again and again, such as a generator test or a blood pressure reading. Every entry has the same fields.

![Making a log](shot:log-editor)

1. Open "Notebooks" and tap "New notebook".
2. Choose "Log" under "Start from", or a ready-made one under "Starter logs".
3. Tap "Add field" for each thing to record.
4. Set its "Label" and "Type": text, number, yes or no, choice or place.
5. Optional: set a "Unit" for a number, or "Choices" for a choice (one per line, first is the default).
6. Optional: set "Due again" so the log shows when it's due or overdue.
7. Tap "Create".

When, text, place and photos are always there.

## Add an entry to a log

1. Tap "Add entry" in the log, or a row under "Log again".
2. Change what's different. Number, choice and yes or no start from your last entry.
3. Tap "Save". Cache says Saved to and the log's name, with "Undo".

![Adding a log entry](shot:log-quick-add)

The top of a log shows when it was "LAST DONE".

- Once a log has entries, you can't change a field's type. Use "Hide field" to stop showing one. Its old values are kept.

## Notes

A Notes notebook is for plans, checklists and lists you keep updating.

![A note being edited](shot:notebook-entry)

1. Open the notebook and tap "New entry".
2. Optional: type a "Title (optional)".
3. Write in the body. Type `- [ ]` and a space to make a checkbox.
4. Optional: tap "Add photo".
5. Tap "Done".

To find a note later, use "Search" on the Notebooks screen, or the Home search box.

## The Places notebook

The Places notebook holds your saved places and shows them on the map.

![The Places notebook](shot:places-list)

1. [Open "Notebooks" and tap "Places"](cache://notebooks/places).
2. Tap a place to open it. Press and hold one to edit it.
3. Tap "Add place" (a plus icon and the words) to add one.

Each place has its own marker colour. Tap a marker to show or hide the place on the map. A hidden place has an outlined marker with a slash.

The first row is always "Where I am", labelled "Follows the phone". You can't delete or move it. It has its own "Weather and alerts" switch and gear, and a marker toggle.

You can also save a place from the map. Press and hold the map and choose "Save", or tap "Save place" on a place's card.

## Add or edit a place

Add place and Edit place are the same screen.

![Editing a place](shot:places-edit)

1. In the Places notebook, tap "Add place". To edit, press and hold a place.
2. Search an address, place or coordinates, or tap the map to move the pin.
3. Type a "Name".
4. Choose a "Marker colour". There are seven, none red. If you don't choose, Cache picks one.
5. Turn "Show on the map" on or off.
6. Turn "Weather and alerts" on to watch the place.
7. Optional: write "Notes" in Markdown, and add "Photos".
8. Tap "Save".

When editing, "Delete place" asks you to confirm.

## Alerts for a place

When "Weather and alerts" is on, a gear appears. It opens "Alerts for" this place.

![A place's alert choices](shot:place-alerts-sheet)

- "Same as your alert setting": follows ["Settings" > "Notifications"](cache://settings/notifications). This is the default.
- "Custom for this place": set "How far out" and the kinds, such as fires, earthquakes and river levels. These pop up even when "Pop up for" is "Serious only" or "None". They're silent in quiet hours unless serious.
- "Off": no alerts for this place.

A place with "Weather and alerts" on is watched. Cache keeps weather, alerts and fires for it.

## The map ring menu

Press and hold any spot on the map to open a ring. The centre shows the place name and coordinates. Tap outside to close it.

![The ring menu](shot:map-ring)

The wedges are:
- "To here": directions to the spot.
- "From here": directions that start at the spot.
- "Add stop": adds the spot to the route on screen.
- "Save": keeps the spot in a notebook.
- "Measure": measures distance from the spot.

Tap the centre to open the spot's coordinate card. It shows latitude and longitude, grid reference, Plus Code and nearest town.

- "Add stop" shows only while a route is shown.

## Directions and stops from the ring

"From here" starts directions at the spot.

1. Press and hold the map and choose "From here".
2. Choose where to go in the search that opens. A chip says the route starts at the spot. Tap it to go back to the phone's position.

If a route is already shown, it is worked out again from the spot.

To add a stop:
1. With a route shown, press and hold the map and choose "Add stop".
2. The spot joins the route after its other stops.

A route can have up to 10 stops. Each is listed under the destination with a remove button.

## Measure distance on the map

1. Press and hold the map and choose "Measure". A dashed line starts at the spot.
2. Tap the map to add each point.
3. Read the running total in the bar, in your units.
4. Tap "Done" to end.

![Measuring on the map](shot:map-measure)

"Undo" removes the last point.

## Save a spot to a notebook

1. Press and hold the map and choose "Save". The ring shows your notebooks, most used first.
2. Tap a notebook. Its entry form opens at that spot. For "Places" it is the Add place screen with the pin there.

![The notebook ring](shot:map-notebook-ring)

The ring holds at most six wedges: five notebooks and "More". "More" turns to the next page, for example "2 of 3". Tap the back arrow in the centre to go back.

## Write with Markdown

Notebook entries are written in Markdown. There are no formatting buttons. You type marks and the text formats as you type.

![The Markdown cheat sheet](shot:markdown-cheatsheet)

- Headings: start a line with `#` and a space.
- Bold: two asterisks round the words. Italic: one. Strikethrough: two tildes.
- Lists: start lines with `-` and a space, or `1.` and a space.
- Checkboxes: `- [ ]` and a space. `- [x]` is ticked.
- Quotes: start a line with `>` and a space.
- Code: backticks round the words.
- Links: words in square brackets, then the address in round brackets.
- Tables: separate columns with `|`.
- Link to another entry: its name in double square brackets.

Tap the small "?" in the editor for a one-screen cheat sheet.

## Private notebooks

Mark a notebook or entry "Private" to leave it out of sync and sharing.

![Edit notebook](shot:notebook-edit)

1. Open the notebook and tap "Edit notebook".
2. Turn on "Private". You can also set it on an entry.

A private notebook says "Left out of sharing and sync unless you choose to include it."

To include it:
1. Open "Edit notebook" on the private notebook.
2. Turn on "Include in sync and sharing". It's off by default.

- Private doesn't hide a notebook from the assistant. Once Cache is open, it can read everything in your notebooks.
- Private is not a lock. Keep a screen lock on your phone.
- Obsidian and Logseq folders still get private notebooks. See Write notebooks to Obsidian or Logseq.

Some starter logs, such as medication and health, start private. You can switch it off.

## Write notebooks to Obsidian or Logseq

Cache can keep your entries as Markdown files in a folder, for Obsidian or Logseq to open.

![Connections](shot:connections)

1. Open "Notebooks", open its menu and tap "Connections".
2. Under "Obsidian" or "Logseq", tap "Connect".
3. For Logseq, choose "Logseq with a folder of Markdown files" or "Logseq 2".
4. Choose the folder.

On Android, Cache uses the system folder picker and keeps the permission you give. On iPhone, it keeps the folder you pick as a bookmark.

What you get:
- Obsidian: Markdown files in the folder. Open it as a vault. Changes in either place update the other.
- Logseq with Markdown files (0.10 and earlier): blocks on the journal pages. Open it as a graph. Changes go both ways.
- "Logseq 2": Cache writes Markdown for Logseq to import. Changes made in Logseq stay there. The row says "export only".

Cache writes your text as you typed it. Each entry's title, date and place go in its properties. Photos are saved too.

- Private notebooks are written like any other, with a private mark in their properties.
- If a Logseq graph isn't Markdown, Cache says it can write to Markdown graphs only.

## Manage a connected folder

The folder's row shows its name and a status: Synced and the time, "Syncing...", "Paused." or "Can't reach the folder."

The row's menu has "Sync now", "Pause" or "Resume", and "Disconnect".

- If an entry changed in both places, a card appears. Pick "Keep Cache's version" or "Keep the file's version". The other is kept as a copy.
- If a file was removed in the folder, pick "Keep in Cache" or "Delete here too".
- If Cache can't read a file, it leaves it alone. Tap "Ignore".

Your other devices are on the same "Connections" screen. See Sharing and sync: choose what to share in chapter 3.

## Ask Cache to add to a notebook

You can tell Cache to log something in "Ask", for example: add a generator test, 20 minutes, under load. It can add to your Journal and log notebooks.

![The confirm card](shot:ask-confirm-card)

1. Ask in plain words.
2. Read the card Cache shows. It says "Add to Journal", or Add to and the log's name, with the details.
3. Tap the add button. "Not now" cancels.

Nothing changes until you tap. To fix a mistake first, tell Cache what to change. It replaces the card.

- If you have no log for it, Cache says so and points you to "Notebooks". It doesn't make logs. Use "New notebook".
