# 2. Using Cache

## Home

"Home" is the first screen. It gives you weather, alerts and shortcuts at a glance.

![The Home screen](shot:home)

From the top it shows:
- the search box, "Search Cache or ask anything";
- the "Set up Cache" card, on a fresh install until it is done;
- the "Weather and alerts" card;
- your "Shortcuts".

The gear at the top right opens "Settings".

The "Weather and alerts" card shows the temperature and any active alerts. A red triangle is a warning. A fire icon is a fire, with its distance. Tap the card to open "Weather".

- If it says "Not set up yet", tap "Turn on weather".
- If you're offline, it shows the last data and its age.

## Search Cache from Home

The search box finds things on your phone, or starts a chat.

![Home search results](shot:home-search)

1. Tap "Search Cache or ask anything" and type.
2. Read the list. It searches your Library, Notebooks, Places and addresses, and Maps layers. Best match is first.
3. Tap a row to open it. Each row shows where it comes from.

The first row is always "Ask Cache:" and what you typed. Tap it, or press the send key, to start a chat with that text.

If nothing matches, "Nothing found" shows under that row. Cache doesn't guess or autocomplete.

## Edit Home

You choose which blocks Home shows and in what order.

![The Edit Home screen](shot:edit-home)

1. On "Home", scroll down and tap "Edit Home".
2. Use a block's switch to show or hide it.
3. Drag a row by its handle to move it.

"Reset to default" puts every block back. Your shortcuts are kept.

## Add a shortcut to Home

A shortcut puts something you use often on "Home". Use "Add to Home" on:
- a module: tap and hold its icon in "Modules";
- a place: tap it in "Maps", then "Add to Home" on its card;
- a Library item: press and hold it in "Library";
- a Library page: "More" > "Add to Home" in the reader;
- a chat: tap and hold it in the chat list.

![A module's options](shot:modules-hold-menu)

## Move or remove a shortcut

1. On "Home", tap and hold the shortcut.
2. Choose "Move earlier", "Move later" or "Remove from Home".

![A shortcut's options](shot:home-shortcut-menu)

- If a shortcut says "That chat is gone" or "That page is not on this phone any more", remove it.

## The bar and Modules

The bar at the bottom starts with "Home" and ends with "Modules". Between them are up to three modules you pin. At first they are "Ask", "Maps" and "Library".

![The Modules screen](shot:modules)

"Modules" lists every module: "Ask", "Library", "Maps", "Videos", "Weather", "Notebooks" and "Settings". A dot under a name means it is on the bar.

To pin or unpin a module:
1. In "Modules", tap and hold its icon.
2. Tap "Pin to bar" or "Unpin from bar".

- If the bar is full, Cache says "The bar is full (3 max)". Tap the row for the module to take off, for example "Replace Library", or tap "Cancel".

For bigger icons, go to ["Settings" > "Appearance"](cache://settings/appearance) and choose "Large" under "Module icons".

## Select several items, and press and hold

Lists have no tick boxes until you ask for them.

![Regions with Select on](shot:regions-select)

To use an item's menu:
1. Press and hold the item. You feel a short buzz and it lifts.
2. Pick an action. For a Library item: "Check for update", "Change icon", "Add to Home" or "Delete".

To act on several items:
1. Tap "Select" in the list's header.
2. Tick the items.
3. Tap the button at the bottom, for example "Download 2 · 730 MB". Then confirm.

Every press-and-hold action is also in your phone's accessibility actions menu.

Delete always asks first. The pop-up shows what it frees and says "You can download it again later." Tap the red "Delete" to go ahead.

## Ask a question

"Ask" answers questions using your phone's sources. It runs on your phone.

![The Ask screen](shot:ask)

1. Open "Ask" from the bar. You can also type in the Home search box and tap "Ask Cache:".
2. If the title says "No model", tap it and choose a model first.
3. Type in "Ask anything" and tap "Send".
4. Wait. You see "Thinking…" or "Searching your sources…". Tap "Stop" to stop it.

Cache looks in your Library, libraries, downloaded places and maps, and weather data. If you set it up, it also searches the web when you're online.

To start fresh, tap "New chat".

- If Cache is still starting, you see "Starting…". The question is answered when it's ready.
- If you see "Cache's search couldn't start, so this wasn't answered.", tap "Try again".

## Ask with your location

Cache uses your location in "Ask" whenever your phone allows it. It never asks.

![The + menu in Ask](shot:ask-plus-menu)

To turn it off for your questions:
1. Tap the + button left of the box where you type.
2. Turn off "Use my location".

- If the phone blocks location, the switch is greyed out. It says "Blocked in the phone's settings" or "The phone's location is switched off".

## Open a route from Ask

When the assistant works out a route, the answer has an "Open navigation" button.

![An answer with Open navigation](shot:ask-open-navigation)

1. Tap "Open navigation". The route opens in "Maps".
2. Tap "Start" to be guided.

- If you see "Navigation unavailable: this route snapshot is no longer available.", ask for directions again.
- If you're already guiding, Cache says "Finish or end the route first".

## Switch or unload the model in Ask

The model name at the top of "Ask" is a menu.

![The model menu in Ask](shot:ask-model-menu)

1. Tap the model name.
2. Choose one:
   - "Switch model…" to pick another model you downloaded;
   - "Unload: free the memory" to unload it;
   - "Manage models" to open the Models screen.

The menu also shows how much memory the model uses.

- If no model is loaded, the title says "No model". Tap it to choose one.

## Sources and citations

Answers show where they came from.

![An answer with its sources](shot:ask-sources)

- Numbers like [1] point to a source.
- A line under the answer says how many sources, for example "1 source". Tap it for the list.
- Each source shows its number, title and a short passage.
- A Library source has "Open the article". Tap it to read the page.

"Not from your sources" means the answer came from nothing on your phone or a search. Check it before you rely on it.

The assistant can be wrong, even with sources. For anything about your safety, read the source.

- If you see "No answer was produced.", ask again in other words, or tap "New chat".

## Report this answer

Use this when an answer is wrong, made up or harmful.

![The report sheet](shot:ask-report)

1. Under the answer, tap "Report this answer".
2. Choose "Offensive or harmful", "Wrong or made up" or "Something else".
3. Optional: type in "What was wrong (optional)".
4. Tap "Save report".

The report holds the question, the answer and the model. It stays on your phone.

Cache says "Report saved on this phone. Find it in Settings > Assistant." That page is now "Cache model and web search".

## See or share reports

1. Go to ["Settings" > "Cache model and web search" > "Reported answers"](cache://settings/assistant).
2. Tap "Share" next to a report, or "Share all reports".
3. Tap "Remove" to delete one.

## Find and open a chat

Every chat is saved on your phone, even after a restart.

![The chat list](shot:chat-list)

1. Tap "Chats" at the top left of "Ask".
2. Type in "Search chats" to find one by its words.
3. Tap a chat to open it. Tap "New chat" to start another.

Chats are grouped as "Pinned", "Today", "Yesterday", "Previous 7 days" and then by month.

## Rename, pin, share or delete a chat

1. In the chat list, tap and hold the chat.
2. Choose one:
   - "Rename", then type a name and tap "Save";
   - "Pin" or "Unpin" (pinned chats stay on top);
   - "Share as Markdown";
   - "Add to Home";
   - "Delete".

![A chat's options](shot:chat-menu)

After a delete, tap "Undo" on "Chat deleted" to bring it back.

To share the open chat, tap "Share chat" at the top of "Ask".

To delete all chats, go to ["Settings" > "Cache model and web search" > "Delete all conversations"](cache://settings/assistant) and tap "Delete all". This can't be undone.

## Search the map

Search finds a place, town, address or coordinates.

1. Open "Maps".
2. Tap "Search places or coordinates" and type.
3. Tap a result. The map moves there and the place card opens.

- If you see "No places on this phone.", tap "Get a region".
- If you see "No places downloaded here.", tap "Get this area".
- If you see "Getting places for this area…", wait. The search runs again by itself.

The locate button ("Find me") moves the map to you. Tap it again to turn the map with your heading. The chip at the top left shows your coordinates. Tap it to copy them.

## The place card and saving a place

Tap a search result or a place to open its card at the bottom of "Maps".

The card has:
- "Directions": a route from where you are;
- "Save place": keeps it in your Places notebook;
- "Add to Home": adds a shortcut;
- "Close".

Press and hold on the map for the ring menu. It has "Add stop" and "Measure". See The map ring menu in chapter 5.

## Get directions

Directions plan a route from where you are to a place.

1. In "Maps", open a place's card. Or press and hold the map and choose "To here".
2. Tap "Directions".
3. Choose "Drive", "Walk", "Bike" or "Off-road".
4. Check the route, its distance and time.
5. Tap "Start".

Road directions work offline if the region is downloaded.

- If you see "Your position is not known yet. Turn on location to get directions from where you are.", tap "Find me" and allow location.
- If you see "No way found between these places" or "One of these places is outside the downloaded maps", try another way of travel or download the region.
- If part of a route has no road, that part is dashed.

## Off-road directions

Off-road plans a route away from roads.

1. Open a place's card and tap "Directions".
2. Tap "Off-road".
3. Choose "On foot" or "Vehicle".
4. Choose land access:
   - "Stay on open land": public land only;
   - "Usual": the normal choice;
   - "Emergency: any land": ignores land rules.
5. Tap "Start".

"Emergency: any land" warns "Ignores land rules: the route may cross private or closed land." Use it only in an emergency.

Changing a choice plans the route again. Choices reset on restart: "Drive", then "On foot" with "Usual".

- If you see "Off-road needs off-road data for this area", download the region, or choose "Walk" or "Drive".

## Follow a route

After "Start", Cache guides you turn by turn.

- The banner shows the next turn.
- "Rerouting" means you left the route.
- Near the end it says "Arriving", then "You have arrived".
- Tap "Turn the voice off" or "Turn the voice on" to change spoken directions.
- Tap "End" to stop.

Guidance keeps running with the screen locked. On Android a "Cache navigation" notification shows.

- If you refuse notifications, guidance still works.
- If it says "Getting your position", wait for the first GPS fix.

## Map layers and offline maps

The "Layers" button on the right of "Maps" opens the layers sheet.

- Switch a layer on or off. Its line says "Downloaded for offline", "Streams when online" or "Needs a connection".
- Use "Opacity" for a layer laid over the map.
- "Show my position" turns your dot on or off.
- "Download a region" opens "Regions".
- "Get more layers" lists every layer.

Layers stream while you're online and keep what you viewed. To keep a whole area offline, download its region. See Download a region in chapter 1.

- If a layer says "This layer's terms do not allow keeping it offline.", it works online only.

## Library

"Library" is one list of everything you keep for offline reading.

![The Library](shot:library)

Each row has an icon, name, description and size. A badge shows only when something needs attention:
- "Update ready";
- "Getting ready", while Cache opens a library.

The guide that comes with Cache shows "Built in". Your own libraries show "Your own".

- Tap a row to open it full screen.
- Press and hold a row, or tap "..." in the opened item, for "Check for update", "Change icon", "Add to Home" and "Delete".
- Tap "Add to Library" for more. See Add to your Library in chapter 1.

"Change icon" picks a picture from your phone.

The built-in guide has no "Delete". Anything else asks first and shows what it frees.

## The reader

The reader opens a Library item.

- "Search this ZIM" searches inside the open work.
- "Find in page" finds words, with "Previous" and "Next".
- "Back", "Forward" and "Main page" are at the bottom.
- "More" > "Add to Home" puts the page on "Home".

- If you see "This ZIM is still being indexed. Text search works when that is done.", you can still read pages.
- If you see "That link is on the web. It is not available offline.", the link goes outside the work.

## Videos

"Videos" plays videos from PeerTube sites. Open it from "Modules".

![The Videos screen](shot:videos-list)

To add a site:
1. Tap "Add an instance".
2. Type the address, for example tube.example.org.
3. Tap "Add".

To watch:
1. Tap the instance.
2. Choose "Latest" or "Channels", or search.
3. Tap a video, then "Play".

## The video player

In the player:
- Tap the speed button (it shows 1x) to pick 0.5x to 2x. "Normal" is 1x.
- Tap "Full screen" for the video alone. Wide video turns landscape and the system bars hide.
- Tap the screen to show the controls.
- Back, or "Leave full screen", leaves full screen.

## Save a video for offline

1. On the video, tap "Download".
2. Pick a size. Smaller sizes save space.
3. When it says "Saved on this phone", tap "Play saved copy" to watch with no connection.

![Saving a video](shot:video-download)

Saved videos are under "Saved on this phone" at the top of "Videos".

To remove them, delete them in "Videos", or go to ["Settings" > "Storage" > "Videos and weather"](cache://settings/storage).

- If a download stopped, tap "Continue".

## Log in to a video site

You only need this for videos a site keeps behind a login.

1. Open the instance and tap "Account and details".
2. Under "Log in (optional)", enter "User name" and "Password". Add the "Two-factor code" if asked.
3. Tap "Log in".

The same sheet has "Log out" and "Remove this instance".

## Weather

"Weather" shows the forecast and alerts for the places you watch. Open it from "Modules", or tap the "Weather and alerts" card on "Home".

![The Weather screen](shot:weather)

- What's already on the phone shows at once, with its age.
- When you open Cache and the last update is over 30 minutes old, it updates.
- The box at the top says when data was last updated. Tap "Update now", or pull the screen down.
- "Happening nearby" lists alerts, fires and other sources you switched on.
- With more than one place, tap a place's chip to switch.

Tap any item for details. "Ask about this" starts a chat about what it means for you.

The tune button ("Weather and alerts settings") opens the source settings.

- If you're offline, Cache shows the last data and its age.

## Weather messages and alerts

Every alert is listed in "Weather", whatever you chose under "Pop up for" in "Notifications".

- If an alert notification opens Cache, it goes straight to that alert.
- If it says "That alert has ended or is no longer in the data.", the alert is over.
- If you see "Waiting for your position." the phone doesn't know where it is yet. Wait for it.
- If you see "Weather is off." or "No place is being watched.", tap "Turn on weather" or "Choose a place instead".
