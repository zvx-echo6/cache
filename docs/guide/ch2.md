# 2. Using Cache

## Home

"Home" is the first screen. It gives you weather, alerts and shortcuts at a glance.

![The Home screen](shot:home)

From the top it shows:
- the search box, "Search Cache or ask anything". With no model on the device it says "Search Cache";
- the "Set up Cache" card, on a fresh install until it is done;
- the "Weather and alerts" card;
- your "Shortcuts".

The gear at the top right opens "Settings".

The "Weather and alerts" card shows the temperature and any active alerts. A red triangle is a warning. A fire icon is a fire, with its distance. Tap the card to open "Weather".

- If it says "Not set up yet", tap "Turn on weather".
- If it says "Weather is off", every source is off. Tap "Turn on". It opens ["Settings" > "Weather and alerts"](cache://settings/weather).
- If you're offline, it shows the last data and its age.

## New version banner and What's new

When a newer Cache is out, "Home" shows a banner.

- On Android it says "New version available" with "Open Play Store".
- On iPhone it says "New version available: open TestFlight" with "Open TestFlight".

Swipe the banner away to dismiss it. It stays gone after a restart. It comes back when an even newer build is out.

To see what changed, open "Library" > "Cache Documentation" and tap "What's new in [version]". See Cache Documentation.

## Search Cache from Home

The search box finds things on your device, or starts a chat.

![Home search results](shot:home-search)

1. Tap "Search Cache or ask anything" and type.
2. Read the list. It searches your Library, Notebooks, Places and addresses, and Maps layers. Best match is first.
3. Tap a row to open it. Each row shows where it comes from.

A match inside a PDF shows its page, for example `p. 47`, and opens the PDF at that page.

The first row is "Ask Cache:" and what you typed. Tap it, or press the send key, to start a chat with that text.

If nothing matches, "Nothing found" shows under that row. Cache doesn't guess or autocomplete.

With no model on the device, the box says "Search Cache". There is no "Ask Cache:" row. If nothing matches, Cache says "Nothing found on this device." and, small under it, "For an answer in words, choose a model." Tap "Choose a model" to get one.

At the foot of the results, a small gray line can say `Still making [library]'s PDFs searchable (340 of 5,768)`. Tap it for that library's status. Tap the close mark ("Hide") to hide it.

## Edit Home

You choose which blocks Home shows and in what order.

![The Edit Home screen](shot:edit-home)

1. On "Home", scroll down and tap "Edit Home".
2. Use a block's switch to show or hide it.
3. Drag a row by its handle to move it.

"Reset to default" puts every block back. Your shortcuts are kept.

## Add a shortcut to Home

A shortcut puts something you use often on "Home". Use "Add to Home" on:
- a module: tap and hold its icon in "Modules";
- a place: tap it in "Maps", then "Add to Home" on its card;
- a Library item: press and hold it in "Library";
- a Library page: "More" > "Add to Home" in the reader;
- a chat: tap and hold it in the chat list.

![A module's options](shot:modules-hold-menu)

## Move or remove a shortcut

1. On "Home", tap and hold the shortcut.
2. Choose "Move earlier", "Move later" or "Remove from Home".

![A shortcut's options](shot:home-shortcut-menu)

- If a shortcut says "That chat is gone" or "That page is not on this device any more", remove it.

## The bar and Modules

The bar at the bottom starts with "Home" and ends with "Modules". Between them are up to three modules you pin. At first they are "Ask", "Maps" and "Library".

With no model on the device, "Ask" leaves the bar. In "Modules" it is grayed, with the line "Needs a model". While a model downloads, the line reads "Model downloading 40%". Your pin is kept, so "Ask" comes back to the bar when a model arrives.

![The Modules screen](shot:modules)

"Modules" lists every module: "Ask", "Library", "Maps", "Videos", "Weather", "Notebooks" and "Settings". A dot under a name means it is on the bar.

To pin or unpin a module:
1. In "Modules", tap and hold its icon.
2. Tap "Pin to bar" or "Unpin from bar".

- If the bar is full, Cache says "The bar is full (3 max)". Tap the row for the module to take off, for example "Replace Library", or tap "Cancel".

For bigger icons, go to ["Settings" > "Appearance"](cache://settings/appearance) and choose "Large" under "Module icons".

## Tablets

On a tablet Cache changes its layout. The screens are the same.

1. Hold the tablet upright. Cache uses the bottom bar, as on a phone.
2. Turn it sideways. Cache uses a rail at the side instead.
3. On the rail, tap "Hide menu" at its top to hide it. Tap "Show menu" at the top left of the page to bring it back.

Settings, Regions, Notebooks, Library and the guide contents show a list beside the open page.
1. Tap "Hide list" at the top of the list to hide it.
2. Tap the button left of the page's title to show it again. It reads "Show Settings", "Show Regions", "Show Notebooks", "Show Library" or "Show contents".

- Before you choose anything, the page says "Choose an item" ("Choose a section" in the guide). The list can't be hidden then.

## Computers and wide windows

From a window 1280 wide, Cache uses its wide layout. That is a computer's window, or a 12.9-inch iPad Pro held sideways. The screens are the same. The layout fills the room.

1. The list beside a page is wider, so a title and detail fit.
2. A page uses the whole window instead of a narrow strip in the middle.
3. Long text, such as an article or an answer, keeps to a line length that is easy to read.
4. "Library" opens on "Add to Library" while nothing is chosen, instead of "Choose an item".
5. On "Videos" from 1400 wide, saved videos sit two across.

Under 1280 wide the layout is the one in Tablets. Settings, Regions, Notebooks, Library and the guide contents still show a list beside the open page, and you can hide the list.

On a computer the video player shows its keys, such as `Play (Space)`. See The video player.

## Select several items, and press and hold

Lists have no tick boxes until you ask for them.

![Regions with Select on](shot:regions-select)

To use an item's menu:
1. Press and hold the item. You feel a short buzz and it lifts.
2. Pick an action. For a Library item: "Check for update", "Change icon", "Get icon from source", "Add to Home" or "Delete".

To act on several items:
1. Tap "Select" in the list's header.
2. Tick the items.
3. Tap the button at the bottom, for example "Download 2 · 730 MB". Then confirm.

Every press-and-hold action is also in your device's accessibility actions menu.

Delete always asks first. The pop-up shows what it frees and says "You can download it again later." Tap the red "Delete" to go ahead.

## Ask a question

"Ask" answers questions using your device's sources. It runs on your device.

![The Ask screen](shot:ask)

1. Open "Ask" from the bar. You can also type in the Home search box and tap "Ask Cache:".
2. If "Ask" says "Ask needs a model on this device.", tap "Choose a model" first. If the title says "No model", tap it and choose a model.
3. Type in "Ask anything" and tap "Send".
4. Follow the progress. While Cache works, one line says what it is doing, such as searching your library, reading a source or calculating, and each finished step stays above it with a check. When the answer starts to appear, the steps fold into one line such as "3 steps"; tap it to show or hide them. "Answer delivered" marks completion. Tap "Stop" to stop it.

Cache looks in your Library, libraries, downloaded places and maps, and weather data. If you set it up, it also searches the web when you're online.

To start fresh, tap "New chat".

- If Cache is still starting, you see "Starting…". The question is answered when it's ready.
- If you see "Cache's search couldn't start, so this wasn't answered.", tap "Try again".

## Ask with your location

Cache uses your location in "Ask" whenever your device allows it. It never asks.

![The + menu in Ask](shot:ask-plus-menu)

To turn it off for your questions:
1. Tap the + button left of the box where you type.
2. Turn off "Use my location".

- If the device blocks location, the switch is grayed out. It says "Blocked in the device's settings" or "The device's location is switched off".

## Open a route from Ask

When the assistant works out a route, the answer has an "Open navigation" button.

![An answer with Open navigation](shot:ask-open-navigation)

1. Tap "Open navigation". The route opens in "Maps".
2. Tap "Start" to be guided.

- If you see "Navigation unavailable: this route snapshot is no longer available.", ask for directions again.
- If you're already guiding, Cache says "Finish or end the route first".

## Switch or unload the model in Ask

The model name at the top of "Ask" is a menu.

![The model menu in Ask](shot:ask-model-menu)

1. Tap the model name.
2. Choose one:
   - "Switch model…" to pick another model you downloaded;
   - "Unload: free the memory" to unload it;
   - "Manage models" to open the Models screen.

The menu also shows how much memory the model uses.

- If no model is loaded, the title says "No model". Tap it to choose one.
- If a model is on the device but did not load, see When a model does not load.

## Use Cache with no model

A model is optional. With none on the device, Cache is search only. Everything that searches still works: Library, Notebooks, Places, Maps and the guide. What goes is "Ask", the part that answers in words.

You are in this mode when you never added a model, when you tap "Not now" on the model row of the "Set up Cache" card, or when you delete your last chat model.

What you see:
1. "Ask" leaves the bar. In "Modules" it is grayed, with the line "Needs a model".
2. Open "Ask" from "Modules". It says "Ask needs a model on this device." with "Choose a model". Under it, "Saved chats" lists your old chats.
3. Tap a saved chat to read it. It opens read-only, with the title "Ask" and a back arrow. Where you type it says "Ask is off. Choose a model to carry on." with "Choose a model".
4. Home search says "Search Cache" and has no "Ask Cache:" row. See Search Cache from Home.
5. "Ask about this" on a weather item and "Ask Cache instead" under a guide search are not shown.

![Ask with no model](shot:ask-no-model)

If a model is downloading, the note reads "A model is downloading. Ask turns on when it is ready." and its button is "See download". The "Modules" line reads "Model downloading 40%".

To turn "Ask" on again, tap "Choose a model" in any of those places and download or copy in a model. See Choose a model in chapter 1.

When the first model is on the device, Cache says "Ask is on." Tap "Open" to go to "Ask". Nothing opens by itself.

- If you open a link to "Ask" from the guide, the same note shows, with "Close".
- To delete your last model, see Unload or delete a model in chapter 1. The pop-up warns "Ask will turn off. Saved chats stay readable."

## When a model does not load

This is for a model that is on the device but "Ask" can't use. "Ask" shows one red line at the top and one button.

- `[model] did not load. It is too big for this device.` Tap "Choose a smaller model".
- `[model] did not load. The file may be damaged.` Tap "Delete and download again". A recommended model downloads again after its pop-up. For any other model, Cache opens the Models screen.
- `[model] did not load.` Tap "Choose a model".
- "No model is loaded." Tap the button, which reads `Load [model]`. If no model on the device can be used, it reads "Choose a model".

## Sources and citations

Answers show where they came from.

![An answer with its sources](shot:ask-sources)

- Numbers like [1] point to a source.
- A line under the answer says how many sources, for example "1 source". Tap it for the list.
- Each source shows its number, title and a short passage.
- A Library source has "Open the article". Tap it to read the page.
- A source in a PDF opens the PDF at that page, with the passage highlighted. See Open a cited passage in a PDF.
- A source in an EPUB opens the book at that chapter, with the first words of the passage marked.
- A source from a video's captions opens the video at that moment, with the line marked "Match". See Captions and the transcript.

"Not from your sources" means the answer came from nothing on your device or a search. Check it before you rely on it.

The assistant can be wrong, even with sources. For anything about your safety, read the source.

- If you see "No answer was produced.", ask again in other words, or tap "New chat".

## Report this answer

Use this when an answer is wrong, made up or harmful.

![The report sheet](shot:ask-report)

1. Under the answer, tap "Report this answer".
2. Choose "Offensive or harmful", "Wrong or made up" or "Something else".
3. Optional: type in "What was wrong (optional)".
4. Tap "Save report".

The report holds the question, the answer and the model. It stays on your device.

Cache says "Report saved on this device. Find it in Settings > Assistant." That page is now "Cache model and web search".

## See or share reports

1. Go to ["Settings" > "Cache model and web search" > "Reported answers"](cache://settings/assistant).
2. Tap "Share" next to a report, or "Share all reports".
3. Tap "Remove" to delete one.

## Find and open a chat

Every chat is saved on your device, even after a restart.

![The chat list](shot:chat-list)

1. Tap "Chats" at the top left of "Ask".
2. Type in "Search chats" to find one by its words.
3. Tap a chat to open it. Tap "New chat" to start another.

Chats are grouped as "Pinned", "Today", "Yesterday", "Previous 7 days" and then by month.

## Rename, pin, share or delete a chat

1. In the chat list, tap and hold the chat.
2. Choose one:
   - "Rename", then type a name and tap "Save";
   - "Pin" or "Unpin" (pinned chats stay on top);
   - "Share as Markdown";
   - "Add to Home";
   - "Delete".

![A chat's options](shot:chat-menu)

After a delete, tap "Undo" on "Chat deleted" to bring it back.

To share the open chat, tap "Share chat" at the top of "Ask".

To delete all chats, go to ["Settings" > "Cache model and web search" > "Delete all conversations"](cache://settings/assistant) and tap "Delete all". This can't be undone.

## Search the map

Search finds a place, town, address or coordinates.

![Map search results](shot:maps-search)

1. Open "Maps".
2. Tap "Search places or coordinates" and type.
3. Tap a result. The map moves there and the place card opens.

- If you see "No places on this device.", tap "Get a region".
- If you see "No places downloaded here.", tap "Get this area".
- If you see "Getting places for this area…", wait. The search runs again by itself.

The locate button ("Find me") moves the map to you. Tap it again to turn the map with your heading. The chip at the top left shows your coordinates. Tap it to copy them.

## The place card and saving a place

Tap a search result or a place to open its card at the bottom of "Maps".

![A place card](shot:place-card)

The card has:
- "Directions": a route from where you are;
- "Save place": keeps it in your Places notebook;
- "Add to Home": adds a shortcut;
- "Close".

When the place has a Wikipedia summary, the card shows it with "Read about it".
- Tap "Read about it". The button says "Opening…", then the article opens in your Wikipedia.
- A place with no Wikipedia match shows no summary and no button.
- If Wikipedia is not on the device, the card shows "Get Wikipedia" and "Wikipedia is not on this device." Tap "Get Wikipedia" to open "Add to Library" and choose a Wikipedia to download.
- If you see "That article is not in your Wikipedia. Update Wikipedia in Library.", tap "Open Library".

Press and hold on the map for the ring menu. It has "Add stop" and "Measure". See The map ring menu in chapter 5.

## Get directions

Directions plan a route from where you are to a place.

![The directions panel](shot:directions-panel)

1. In "Maps", open a place's card. Or press and hold the map and choose "To here".
2. Tap "Directions".
3. Choose "Drive", "Walk", "Bike" or "Off-road".
4. Check the route, its distance and time.
5. Tap "Start".

Road directions work offline if the region is downloaded.

- If you see "Your position is not known yet. Turn on location to get directions from where you are.", tap "Find me" and allow location.
- If you see "No way found between these places" or "One of these places is outside the downloaded maps", try another way of travel or download the region.
- If part of a route has no road, that part is dashed.

## Off-road directions

Off-road plans a route away from roads.

![Off-road directions](shot:directions-offroad)

1. Open a place's card and tap "Directions".
2. Tap "Off-road".
3. Choose "On foot" or "Vehicle".
4. Choose land access:
   - "Stay on open land": public land only;
   - "Usual": the normal choice;
   - "Emergency: any land": ignores land rules.
5. Tap "Start".

"Emergency: any land" warns "Ignores land rules: the route may cross private or closed land." Use it only in an emergency.

Changing a choice plans the route again. Choices reset on restart: "Drive", then "On foot" with "Usual".

- If you see "Off-road needs off-road data for this area", download the region, or choose "Walk" or "Drive".

## Follow a route

After "Start", Cache guides you turn by turn.

![Following a route](shot:guidance)

- The banner shows the next turn.
- "Rerouting" means you left the route.
- Near the end it says "Arriving", then "You have arrived".
- Tap "Turn the voice off" or "Turn the voice on" to change spoken directions.
- Tap "End" to stop.

Guidance keeps running with the screen locked. On Android a "Cache navigation" notification shows.

- If you refuse notifications, guidance still works.
- If it says "Getting your position", wait for the first GPS fix.

## Map layers and offline maps

The "Layers" button on the right of "Maps" opens the layers sheet.

![The layers sheet](shot:layers-sheet)

- Switch a layer on or off. Its line says "Downloaded for offline", "Streams when online" or "Needs a connection".
- Use "Opacity" for a layer laid over the map.
- "Show my position" turns your dot on or off.
- "Download a region" opens "Regions".
- "Get more layers" lists every layer.
- Under "My layers", "Add from a file" adds a file of places or repeaters. See Add a repeater list from a file in chapter 5.

Layers stream while you're online and keep what you viewed. To keep a whole area offline, download its region. See Download a region in chapter 1.

- If a layer says "This layer's terms do not allow keeping it offline.", it works online only.

## Library

"Library" has four rows. Each one says in a line or two what it holds, and a fix shows in color, for example "Update ready for 1" or "9 unsorted".

![The Library](shot:library)

- "ZIM Collections": the ZIMs from Kiwix, such as Wikipedia and medical guides.
- "Bookshelves": your books, shelved by the Cache Decimal System.
- "Catalog": every search index on this device, with "Pause all" and "Resume".
- "Cache Documentation": this guide, "What's new in [version]" and "Credits and licenses".

At the top, a bar shows while Cache makes search ready in the background. See Search inside PDFs, EPUBs and captions.

Tap "Add to Library" at the foot to add anything. See Add to your Library in chapter 1.

On a tablet the Library's rows stay at the left and each one opens beside them. On a wide screen, the list you are in and the item you chose sit side by side. A line at the top of each page, for example `Library › Bookshelves › CDS 200`, shows where you are. Tap a part of it to go back there. Tap "Hide list" for more room, and "Show list" to bring the list back.

## ZIM Collections

"ZIM Collections" lists your ZIMs. Each row has an icon, name, description and size.

1. Open "Library" and tap "ZIM Collections".
2. Tap a row to open it in the reader.
3. Press and hold a row, or tap "..." in the opened item, for "Check for update", "Change icon", "Get icon from source", "Add to Home" and "Delete".

A badge shows only when something needs attention, for example "Update ready". Tap it to update.

Under a row, a line can say how its search is going, for example `Making PDFs searchable · 340 of 5,768`. Tap that line to open it in "Catalog", where "Pause all" and "Resume" are.

"Change icon" picks a picture from your device. "Get icon from source" fetches the picture again from where the ZIM came from (Kiwix), or reads the one inside the ZIM; it says "No icon found at the source" when there is none. "Delete" asks first and shows what it frees.

- If you see "No ZIM Collections yet. Add Wikipedia, medical guides or survival manuals.", tap "Add to Library".

## Bookshelves

"Bookshelves" shows your books on numbered shelves, the Cache Decimal System, CDS for short. There are ten classes, for example "CDS 200 · Medicine and Health", and each has nine divisions, for example "CDS 210 · First aid and emergency care". Every shelf shows, empty ones too.

1. Open "Library" and tap "Bookshelves".
2. At the top, choose how the shelves look: "List", "Drawers" or "Spines". Cache keeps your choice on this device.
3. Tap a class to see its divisions, then a division to see its books.

Every book has a call number, for example `CDS 210-002`: its division, then its place in the order books arrived. A number never changes and is never used again. A moved book gets a new number in its new division. If two of your devices give out the same number at the same moment, the later book shows `.1` after it, for example `CDS 210-002.1`.

To jump to a shelf or a book, type its number in "Go to a number, such as 530". It takes a class (500), a division (530) or a call number (530-002).

- If you see "No books yet.", tap "Add to Library".
- If you see "No shelf has that number. Try one such as 530.", check the number.

While books are being made searchable, a bar says so, for example "Making 3 books searchable". Tap it to open "Catalog".

## Unsorted and Place it

Books Cache was not sure about wait in "Unsorted" at the top of "Bookshelves". It shows only while it holds books.

1. On "Bookshelves", tap "Unsorted", then "Place them".
2. Each book shows its best guesses, the best one first. Tap one to place the book there.
3. Or tap "All divisions" and pick any division.

Cache says where the book went and its new call number, for example `Placed in CDS 350 · Beekeeping as CDS 350-004.` Tap "Undo" to take it back.

To place every book at its best guess at once, tap "Place all best guesses". Tap "Undo" to take back the whole lot.

A book that is not searchable yet can be placed too. Your choice is saved and helps Cache learn.

## A book's page

Tap a book on a shelf to open its page. At the top is its catalog card: its call number, title, and author and edition when known. Under it:
- "Subject": its division, and "Filed by Cache" or "Placed by you". Tap "Move" to put it in another division;
- "PDF": its size, and where it is when Cache knows;
- "Search": "Searchable here", or how far Cache is;
- "Stored": how much smaller Cache made it, when it did.

Tap "Delete book" to delete it. See Delete a book.

## Delete a book

"Delete book" has two separate choices.

1. Open the book's page and tap "Delete book".
2. Choose one:
   - "Remove from this device": the book leaves this device only. Cache first asks your other devices whether one has it. If none does, nothing is removed;
   - "Delete from all your devices": the book, its search index and its call number go from every linked device. This cannot be undone.

- If a choice says "Not available on this device yet.", this device can't do it yet.

## Catalog

"Catalog" lists every search index on this device, grouped: "Your books", "ZIM Collections", "Your own" and "Maps and places". It is the one place for pausing and fixing them.

1. Open "Library" and tap "Catalog".
2. Read each row: its name, its kind (for example PDFS or PAGES), how it stands, and how it syncs.
3. Use the button on a row when it has one: "Pause", "Resume", "Try again" or "Free space".

"Pause all" stops all the work, and "Resume" goes on. A sync line can say "Not synced: sharing is off" or "Not synced yet". See Sharing and sync: set up and link devices in chapter 3.

Tap one of your own libraries to see what it holds, with its "..." menu.

## Cache Documentation

"Cache Documentation" holds this guide, what changed in your version, and the credits.

1. Open "Library" and tap "Cache Documentation".
2. Tap "The Cache guide" to read this guide. It is built in and can't be deleted.
3. Tap "What's new in [version]" to see what changed. It shows only when lines were written for that version.
4. Tap "Credits and licenses" to see what Cache is built on. See Credits and licenses.

## The reader

The reader opens a Library item.

- "Search this ZIM" searches inside the open work. Matches can be pages, PDFs, EPUB chapters and video captions in it.
- "Find in page" finds words, with "Previous" and "Next".
- "Back", "Forward" and "Main page" are at the bottom. "Back" and "Forward" move through the pages you read.
- The arrow at the top left leaves the reader. Its label reads "Close [library name]".
- "More" > "Add to Home" puts the page on "Home".
- A link to a PDF opens the PDF reader over the whole window. See The PDF reader.
- A link to an EPUB opens its first chapter in the reader.
- A link to a video in the work plays in the video player, with its captions. See The video player.

- If you see "This ZIM is still being indexed. Text search works when that is done.", you can still read pages.
- If you see "That link is on the web. It is not available offline.", the link goes outside the work.

## Search inside PDFs, EPUBs and captions

Search in a library reaches more than its pages. It finds words inside the PDFs and EPUB books in a ZIM, and in the captions of its videos. Nothing to switch on and nothing to start. It is always on.

![A PDF passage and words said in a video in Home search](shot:search-pdf-hits)

1. Add a Library item that holds PDFs, EPUBs or videos with captions. See Add to your Library in chapter 1.
2. Leave Cache open. It makes them searchable in the background, a little at a time, so your device stays cool.
3. Search as usual: the Home search box, "Search this ZIM" in the reader, or a question in "Ask".
4. Tap a result. A PDF opens at the page, an EPUB at the chapter, and a video at the moment. The passage is highlighted or marked.

At the top of "Library", a bar shows the progress of all of it, for example `Getting search ready · 3 of 9 done`. Under it is what Cache works on now, for example `Army Publishing · PDFs 340 of 5,768`. The number counts libraries and their parts (PDFs, EPUBs, captions). The bar also moves inside a big library, by the files it has read. When it is all done, the bar says `Search ready · 9 of 9` for a moment and goes. The same bar is at the foot of "Settings".

Tap the bar to see the queue: "Running now" with its progress, "Next" in the order they run, and "Done". Each has its own small bar. Tap "Pause all" to stop it all yourself, and "Resume" to go on.

Cache works on one thing at a time. Only that one says `Making PDFs searchable · 340 of 5,768`, under its row in "ZIM Collections" and in "Catalog". The others say "Waiting to make PDFs searchable" (or EPUBs, or captions) until their turn. "Catalog" shows each part of each ZIM on its own row.

When Cache waits, the bar, the row and the menu say why and what to do:
- `Paused · battery low (12%) · plug in to continue`: under 20% and not plugged in. Plug in, and it goes on by itself;
- "Waits for charging (long job)": a long job starts only on charge, or with the battery above half. Once it runs, it goes on until the battery is low;
- "Slowed down · device is warm" or "Paused · device is warm": the work eases off when the device is warm;
- "Slowed down · keeping the device cool" or "Paused · keeping the device cool": the device is not warm yet, but the system says it would get warm, so the work eases off first;
- "Paused · while Ask answers": it waits while "Ask" writes an answer;
- "Stuck · skipped for now · Retry": it made no progress for 5 minutes, so Cache went on with the rest. Tap it in the list to try again;
- "Paused · Cache is in the background": work runs only while Cache is open;
- `Needs 1.2 GB free · Free space`: it needs room. Tap "Free space";
- "Paused by you · Resume": you paused it. Tap "Resume".

In "Catalog", tap "Resume" on a paused row to start it now, or "Pause" to stop it yourself.

If a few PDFs can't be read, a small line says so, for example `12 could not be read`. A scanned PDF is pictures with no text, so search can't read it.

You can search before it finishes. Results from the finished part show. Under the results, a small line says `Still making [library]'s PDFs searchable (340 of 5,768)`.

An index built on one device can reach your others. See Search indexes on linked devices in chapter 3. When another device finished first, Cache says "Another device finished this. Using its index."

To change how fast Cache builds in the background, see Background indexing speed in chapter 3. The one quiet line at the foot of "Settings" shows the overall progress.

- If a video's captions are not indexed yet, a caption search finds nothing in it. Wait and search again.
- For indexing that seems stuck, see An index still building in chapter 6.

## The PDF reader

PDFs open in their own reader. It takes the whole window.

![The PDF reader](shot:pdf-reader)

You can open a PDF from:
- a link in a Library page, or a search result;
- a source in "Ask";
- a file attached to a notebook entry.

1. At the top, the arrow leaves the reader. Its label reads "Close [title]".
2. Beside the title, the page count reads `12 of 340`. Tap it to go to a page.
3. In "Go to page", type the page number and tap "Go". If it is out of range, it says "Pages 1 to 340".
4. Use "Previous page" and "Next page" at the bottom.
5. Tap "Fit width" or "Fit page" at the bottom. The button is named for the mode it switches to.
6. Pinch to zoom, or double tap. If you can't zoom more, a note says "Zoom limited on this device."
7. Tap the page to hide the bars or show them again.
8. Tap a link in the PDF. For a web link, Cache asks "Open this link in your browser? It needs a connection." Tap "Open" or "Cancel".

While it opens:
- "Opening..." shows first.
- "Still opening. This file is 312 MB." has "Cancel".
- "Getting the PDF ready..." has "Cancel".

For a night screen, turn on "Dark pages" in ["Settings" > "Appearance"](cache://settings/appearance). See Appearance in chapter 3.

- If you see "This PDF is locked. Enter its password.", type it in "Password" and tap "Open". "That password is not right." means try again.
- If you see "Not enough space to open this PDF on this device. It needs 340 MB.", tap "Open Storage" and free space.
- If you see "This PDF can't be opened. The file may be damaged.", tap "Try again". For a file from Storage, "Show in Storage" is there too.
- If you see "This file is no longer on this device.", tap "Open Storage", or "Open Library" for a PDF in a library.
- A screen reader says `Page 12 of 340` and then the page's text. A page that is only a picture says `Page 12 of 340. Picture, no text.`

## Find in a PDF

1. In the PDF reader, tap "Find in this PDF" at the top right. On a computer, press Ctrl+F.
2. Type in the field "Find in this PDF". The search runs a page at a time.
3. Read the count: "Searching...", `12 found so far`, `3 of 17` or "No matches".
4. Tap "Next match" or "Previous match" to move.
5. Tap "Close find" when you are done.

Matches are highlighted and the page scrolls to the current one. A new search replaces the old one.

- If the PDF is mixed, a small line says "Scanned pages skipped".
- If the PDF is only scanned pictures, the field says "No searchable text in this PDF."

## Open a cited passage in a PDF

When you open a PDF from a search result or from a source in "Ask", it opens at the cited page. The cited passage is highlighted, and a strip under the title says "Cited passage".

![A cited passage in a PDF](shot:pdf-cited-passage)

1. Read the highlighted passage.
2. Tap the page, or tap "Close" on the strip, to clear the highlight.

If Cache can't place it, the strip says why:
- "Page 47. The exact passage was not found on this page." Tap "Find on other pages" to search the PDF for its opening words.
- "This PDF has 340 pages. Cited page 400 is not here."
- "Page 12. Scanned page, nothing to highlight."
- "This PDF is scanned pictures. Search can't read it."

A cited EPUB opens at its chapter, with the first words of the passage marked.

## Videos

"Videos" lists the videos on this device and the video sites where you find more. Open it from "Modules". Saved videos are in this list now. There is no separate "Saved" tile.

![The Videos list](shot:videos-list)

From the top it shows:
- "Downloading": videos on their way, each with "Pause" or "Resume";
- "On this device": how many videos and how much space, for example `3 videos · 410 MB`. A row has a picture with its length, the title, and a line such as `480p · 230 MB · Captions`. With videos from two or more channels, they are grouped by channel;
- "Video sites": your sites, under the line "Find videos on a site".

If nothing is saved, it says "Nothing on this device yet. Find a video on a site and choose Download."

Tap a saved video to open its page. See The video page.

From 8 saved videos a search field shows. See Search saved videos.

To see only the saved videos, go to ["Settings" > "Storage"](cache://settings/storage) and open "Videos and weather". That list is called "Saved videos". It has no sites.

On a window 1400 wide or more, saved videos sit two across.

## Add a video site

A video site is a PeerTube site. Anyone can run one.

1. Open "Videos".
2. Tap "Add a video site". When a site is already listed, tap "Add" at the right of "Video sites" instead.
3. In the pop-up "Add a video site", type the address, for example tube.example.org. It says "The address of a PeerTube site."
4. Tap "Add". The site opens.

- If you see "That address doesn't look like a PeerTube site.", check the address.
- If you see "Can't reach the video site. Check the connection and try again.", connect and try again.

## Find a video on a site

1. In "Videos", tap the site under "Video sites".
2. Choose "Latest" or "Channels". Or type in "Search this site" and press the search key.
3. Tap a video to open its page.

A result that you already saved says "Saved". One that is downloading says `Downloading 40%`.

- If you see "This site has no videos", the site has none to show.
- If you see `Nothing found for "water"`, try other words.

## The video page

This page is for one video. A video you saved opens with no connection.

1. Tap the picture to play. There is no Play button. The picture plays the saved copy if you have one, and streams it if you don't.
2. Under the picture, read the title and the line with the channel, length and date, for example `Camp Skills · 12:40 · 14 Mar 2026`.
3. Read where the video is: "Not on this device", or `On this device · 480p · 230 MB`, or the download's progress.
4. If it has captions, a line says "Captions: English, Spanish".
5. Tap "More" under a long description to read it all. Tap "Less" to fold it.

The page has "Download" for a video that is not here. A video that is here has "Delete from this device".

If the picture can't play, a line under it says why:
- "A live stream can't be played here."
- "This video has no stream to play yet."
- For a private video, a lock shows on the picture. See Private videos.

- If you see "This video site is not on your list.", the site was removed and you have nothing saved from it.
- If you see "This video has no file to download yet.", try later.

## The video player

The player opens when you tap the picture on the video page.

![The player's settings sheet](shot:video-player)

1. Tap the picture to show or hide the controls. While it plays they hide after 3 seconds. When it is paused they stay.
2. Use "Play" or "Pause", "Back 10 seconds" and "Forward 10 seconds". At the end of the video the button is "Replay". You can also double tap the left or right half of the picture to skip 10 seconds. It flashes "−10 s" or "+10 s".
3. Drag the bar to move through the video. The time shows as the time so far and the length.
4. Tap "Full screen" for the video alone. A wide video turns landscape and the system bars hide. Tap "Leave full screen" or press Back to come out.
5. Tap the gear ("Settings") for "Speed", "Quality" and "Captions".

Under the title the player shows the channel, the length, and "On this device" or "Streaming".

In "Speed" you choose "Normal" or 0.5x up to 2x. In "Quality" a streamed video offers "Auto" and each size the site has. A saved copy shows `Quality: 480p (saved)`. Cache starts a stream at the quality you last chose on this device.

On a computer, the buttons show their keys: `Play (Space)`, `Captions (C)`, `Full screen (F)` and `Leave full screen (Esc)`. The arrow keys skip 5 seconds.

- If you see "This video can't be played.", tap "Try again". If you have a saved copy, tap "Saved copy".
- If you see "This file can't be played.", tap "Delete it and download again".

## Resume a video

Cache remembers where you stopped in each video, on this device only.

1. Open the video and play it again.
2. It starts where you left it. A line says `Resumed at 3:42` for 5 seconds.
3. Tap "Start over" in that line to go back to the start.

A video watched to near its end starts from the beginning next time.

## Captions and the transcript

A video can have captions: ones the site gives, ones saved with it, or ones inside a ZIM's video.

1. Tap the captions button on the picture (it looks like CC). Its tooltip reads "Captions: Off" or "Captions: English". Captions show across the bottom of the picture.
2. To pick a language, long press the captions button (with two or more languages), or tap the gear and "Captions". Choose "Off" or a language, for example `Spanish (automatic)`. Automatic means the site's speech recognition made them.

Cache remembers your choice on this device.

On a wide, tall window (at least 900 wide and 600 high, such as a computer's window), the transcript sits beside the video:
- the heading is "Transcript". With two or more languages it adds the language;
- each line has its time. The line being said is highlighted and kept in view;
- tap a line to jump there.

![The transcript beside the video](shot:video-transcript)

On a wide window that is too short, tap the gear and "Transcript". It opens as a sheet. On a phone there is no transcript pane.

## Search saved videos

You can search the words said in your saved videos.

1. Open "Videos". From 8 saved videos a field shows: "Search saved videos".
2. Type some words. Cache matches titles, channels and the words said in the captions.
3. Tap a caption match. The player opens at that moment with the line marked "Match".

A caption match shows the time and the matching words under the video, for example `1:04  It makes a loop`. A video inside a ZIM shows "In [ZIM title] (on this device)".

If nothing matches, it says `No saved video matches "water".`

Cache builds the caption index in the background. A video whose captions are not indexed yet has no matches.

## Save a video for offline

1. Open the video page and tap "Download".
2. In the sheet "Download", choose a size: "Best 720p", "Smaller 360p" or "Audio only". The size shows on the right. Cache starts on the smallest video size. "Smaller sizes use less space on this device."
3. Read the captions line: "Captions come with it: English, Spanish." or "This video has no captions."
4. Tap the button, for example `Download 360p · 120 MB`.

The video shows under "Downloading" in "Videos" with "Pause" and "Resume". When it is done, the page says "On this device".

- If the sheet says "Not enough room on this device.", tap "Open Storage" and free space. See Storage full in chapter 6.
- If a site doesn't allow it, the page says "[site] doesn't allow downloading this video. You can still watch it online."
- If a caption did not save, the page says "Spanish captions did not save." Tap "Try again". It fetches only the missing captions.
- If the download stopped, tap "Resume".

To delete a video, tap "Delete from this device". The pop-up asks "Delete this video?" and says `It is deleted from this device (230 MB).` Tap "Delete" or "Keep". You can download it again later.

## Sign in to a video site

You only need this for videos a site keeps behind a sign-in.

![Signing in to a video site](shot:video-sign-in)

1. Open the site and tap the account button at the top right. Its tooltip is "Account and details", or your user name once you are signed in.
2. Under "Sign in (optional)", look at what the site offers.
3. If it has its own ways in, tap "Sign in with [name]". The site's page opens inside Cache. Finish there.
4. Or use "Or with your user name and password". Type "User name" and "Password". Add the "Two-factor code" if asked.
5. Tap "Sign in".

The sheet then says "Signed in as [user name]". Tap "Sign out" to sign out.

- If you see "Sign-in with [name] was canceled.", you closed the page. Tap it again.
- If you see "That sign-in took too long or was already used. Try again.", tap it again.
- If you see "The video site needs you to sign in again.", sign in again.

## Private videos

A private video has a lock on its picture.

1. Open the video page. It says "Sign in to [site] to play this video".
2. Tap "Sign in" and sign in. See Sign in to a video site.
3. Tap the picture to play.

Once you sign in, private videos play at any size the site has.

## Remove a video site

1. Open the site and tap the account button.
2. Tap "Remove this video site".
3. If you saved videos from it, choose "Keep videos" or "Remove and delete videos". Or tap "Cancel".

Without saved videos, tap "Remove".

## Weather

"Weather" shows the forecast and alerts for the places you watch. Open it from "Modules", or tap the "Weather and alerts" card on "Home".

![The Weather screen](shot:weather)

- What's already on the device shows at once, with its age.
- When you open Cache and the last update is over 30 minutes old, it updates.
- The card at the top has one line that says when data was last updated, for example "Updated 12 min ago". Tap "Update now", or pull the screen down.
- "Happening nearby" lists alerts, fires and other sources you switched on, the most serious first. Weather Service alerts and "Emergency alerts" from FEMA IPAWS show together. The same alert from both shows once.
- Tap the place's name at the top to open "Places". With more than one place, tap a place there to switch.

Tap any item for details. Under "Forecast", tap a day for its details. "Ask about this" starts a chat about what it means for you.

The tune button ("Weather and alerts settings") opens the source settings.

- If you're offline, Cache shows the last data and its age.

## Weather messages and alerts

Every alert is listed in "Weather", whatever you chose under "Pop up for" in "Notifications".

An emergency alert shows "Event", "Severity", "Issued by", "Area" and "Expires", then "Instructions" or "Details". Severity is written as for Weather Service alerts.

- If an alert notification opens Cache, it goes straight to that alert.
- If it says "That alert has ended or is no longer in the data.", the alert is over.
- If you see "Waiting for your position." the device doesn't know where it is yet. Wait for it.
- If you see "Weather is off." or "No place is being watched.", tap "Turn on weather" or "Choose a place instead".
