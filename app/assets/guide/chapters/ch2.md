# 2. Using Cache

## Home

"Home" is the first screen when Cache opens. It is the first button on the bar at the bottom.

From top to bottom it shows:
- The search box, "Search Cache or ask anything". See the section Search Cache from Home.
- On a fresh install, the "Set up Cache" card. It goes away when its four things are done.
- One "Weather and alerts" card. It shows a weather icon and the temperature. Active alerts show below it with icons: a red warning triangle for a warning, a fire icon for a fire, each with one line of detail. Tap the card to open "Weather".
- "Shortcuts": things you added to Home, such as a place, a Library page, a chat or a module.

The gear at the top right opens "Settings".

The card needs a connection to stay current. Offline it shows the last data with its age, or says that it has expired and needs a connection.

When the card says "Not set up yet", nothing is switched on. Tap "Turn on weather" on the card, or open "Weather".

## Search Cache from Home

The search box at the top of "Home" searches all of Cache and can also start a chat.

1. Tap "Search Cache or ask anything" and type.
2. Results appear as you type, in one tight list, best match first. Cache searches your Library, your Notebooks, Places and addresses, and Maps layers.
3. Each row shows the icon of the part of Cache it comes from. Line two names it, for example "Library".
4. Tap a row to open it.

The first row is always "Ask Cache:" followed by exactly what you typed. Tap it, or press the send key on the keyboard, to start a new chat in "Ask" with that text.

Cache does not guess or autocomplete. If nothing matches, "Nothing found" shows under the Ask Cache row.

This is a search of what is on your phone. The assistant's own search works as before, inside "Ask".

## Edit Home and shortcuts

You can choose what Home shows and in which order.

To change the blocks:
1. On "Home", scroll down and tap "Edit Home".
2. Use the switch next to a block to show or hide it.
3. Drag a row by its handle to move it.
4. "Reset to default" puts every block back, in the first order, all on. Your shortcuts are kept.

To add a shortcut, use "Add to Home" on:
- a module: on the "Modules" screen, tap and hold its icon;
- a place: in "Maps", tap the place, then "Add to Home" on its card;
- a Library item: press and hold it in "Library" and choose "Add to Home", or tap "More" > "Add to Home" in the reader;
- a chat: in "Ask", open the chat list, tap and hold the chat.

To move or remove a shortcut:
1. On "Home", tap and hold the shortcut.
2. Choose "Move earlier", "Move later" or "Remove from Home".

If a shortcut no longer works, Cache says why, for example "That chat is gone" or "That page is not on this phone any more". Remove it from Home.

## The bar and Modules

The bar at the bottom always starts with "Home" and ends with "Modules". Between them are up to three modules you pin. At first these are "Ask", "Maps" and "Library". The bar hides while the keyboard is open.

"Modules" shows every module: "Ask", "Library", "Maps", "Videos", "Weather", "Notebooks" and "Settings". A small dot under a name means it is on the bar. You can pin any of them, including "Notebooks".

- Tap an icon to open the module.
- Tap and hold an icon for its options: "Pin to bar" or "Unpin from bar", and "Add to Home".

To pin a module when the bar already has three:
1. In "Modules", tap and hold the icon and tap "Pin to bar".
2. Cache says "The bar is full (3 max)".
3. Tap the row for the module you want to take off the bar, for example "Replace Library", or tap "Cancel".

To make the icons bigger, go to "Settings" > "Appearance" and choose "Large" under "Module icons".

## Select several items, and press and hold

Cache has no tick boxes on its lists by default. Two things work the same way everywhere.

Press and hold:
1. Press and hold an item. You feel a short vibration and the item lifts.
2. A menu opens with what you can do to that item. For a Library item that is "Check for update", "Change icon", "Add to Home" and "Delete".

Every press-and-hold action is also in your phone's accessibility actions menu.

Select several:
1. Where a list has it, tap "Select" in the header. Tick boxes appear.
2. Tick what you want. The button at the bottom says what it will do, for example "Download 2 · 730 MB" in the regions sheet.
3. Tap the button. A pop-up asks you to confirm.

Press and hold on a row is a shortcut into "Select" in lists that have it.

Delete always asks first. The pop-up says what it frees and how much storage you will have after, and says "You can download it again later." The red "Delete" button does it. "Cancel" leaves things as they are.

## Ask a question

"Ask" is where you talk to the assistant. It runs on your phone.

1. Open "Ask" from the bar, or use the search box on "Home" and tap "Ask Cache:".
2. If the title says "No model", tap it and choose a model first.
3. Type your question in "Ask anything" and tap "Send".
4. If Cache is still starting, the question waits and you see "Starting…". It is answered when Cache is ready.
5. While it works you see "Thinking…" or "Searching your sources…". Tap "Stop" to stop it.

If Cache's search can't start, the answer is "Cache's search couldn't start, so this wasn't answered." Tap "Try again".

Cache looks in your sources before it answers: your Library, your libraries, downloaded places and maps, weather and alerts data, and, if you set it up, web search when you are online.

Location: Cache uses where you are whenever your phone allows it. It never asks in "Ask". To turn it off for your questions:
1. Tap the "+" button to the left of the box where you type.
2. Turn off "Use my location".

If the phone has blocked location for Cache, that switch is greyed out and says why: "Blocked in the phone's settings", or "The phone's location is switched off".

If the assistant works out a route, the answer has an "Open navigation" button. Tap it to open the route in "Maps". If it says "Navigation unavailable: this route snapshot is no longer available.", ask again for directions.

To start fresh, tap "New chat".

## Switch or unload the model in Ask

The name of the loaded model is at the top of "Ask". It has a small down arrow, because it is a menu.

1. Tap the model name.
2. The menu shows the model and about how much memory it uses.
3. Choose one of:
   - "Switch model…": pick another model you already downloaded.
   - "Unload: free the memory": the model leaves memory. No model is loaded until you load one again.
   - "Manage models": opens the Models screen, where you download and delete models.

If no model is loaded, the title says "No model". Tap it to choose one.

## Sources and citations

Answers in "Ask" show where they came from.

- Numbers in the answer, like [1], point to a source.
- Under the answer, a line says how many sources were used, for example "1 source". Tap it to see the list.
- Each source shows its number, title and a short passage. A source from your Library also shows "Open the article": tap it to read the page in the reader.
- "Not from your sources" means the answer did not come from anything on your phone or from a search. Treat it with more care, and check it before you rely on it.

If you see "No answer was produced.", try asking again in other words, or start a "New chat".

The assistant can be wrong, even with sources. For anything that matters to your safety, open the source and read it.

## Report this answer

If an answer is wrong, made up or harmful, you can save a report.

1. Under the answer in "Ask", tap "Report this answer".
2. Choose a reason: "Offensive or harmful", "Wrong or made up" or "Something else".
3. If you like, type in "What was wrong (optional)".
4. Tap "Save report". Cache says "Report saved on this phone. Find it in Settings > Assistant."

That page is now called "Cache model and web search". The report holds the question, the answer and the model. It stays on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

To see or share reports:
1. Go to "Settings" > "Cache model and web search" > "Reported answers".
2. Tap "Share" next to a report to send it yourself, or "Share all reports".
3. Tap "Remove" to delete one.

## Chats: find, rename, pin, share and delete

Every chat is kept on your phone and is still there after a restart.

To open the chat list, tap "Chats" at the top left of "Ask". Chats are grouped as "Pinned", "Today", "Yesterday", "Previous 7 days" and then by month.
- Type in "Search chats" to find one by its words.
- Tap a chat to open it.
- Tap "New chat" to start a new one.

To change a chat, tap and hold it in the list:
- "Rename": type a new name and tap "Save".
- "Pin" or "Unpin": pinned chats stay at the top.
- "Share as Markdown": sends the chat as text through your phone's share sheet.
- "Add to Home": puts a shortcut on "Home".
- "Delete": removes it. Tap "Undo" on the message "Chat deleted" if you change your mind.

To share the chat you have open, tap "Share chat" at the top of "Ask".

To delete every chat at once, go to "Settings" > "Cache model and web search" > "Delete all conversations" and confirm with "Delete all". This cannot be undone.

## Search the map

1. Open "Maps".
2. Tap "Search places or coordinates" at the top.
3. Type a place, a town, an address or coordinates.
4. Tap a result. The map moves there and the place card opens.

What the messages under the results mean:
- "No places on this phone." with "Get a region": no places are downloaded. Tap "Get a region" and download a region.
- "No places downloaded here." with "Get this area": you have places, but not for this area. Tap "Get this area" to download the region there.
- "Getting places for this area…": Cache is fetching places for this area over the connection. The search runs again by itself when they arrive.

Other things on the map:
- The locate button ("Find me") moves the map to you. Tap it again to make the map turn with your heading.
- The chip at the top left shows your coordinates and how accurate they are. Tap it to copy them.
- Dragging the map stops it following you.

You can also search your saved places and addresses from the search box on "Home".

## The place card and saving a place

When you tap a search result or a place, its card opens at the bottom of "Maps".

The card's buttons:
- "Directions": plan a route there from where you are.
- "Save place": keeps the place in your Places notebook.
- "Add to Home": puts the place on "Home" as a shortcut.
- "Close": closes the card.

Press and hold on the map opens the ring menu: "To here", "From here", "Add stop" (while a route is shown), "Save" and "Measure". The centre opens the spot's coordinates. See the section The map ring menu in chapter 5.

Saved places are listed in the Places notebook. Open Notebooks and tap Places. To change a place or its weather setting, see the section Add or edit a place in chapter 5.

## Get directions

1. In "Maps", open a place's card (search for it), or press and hold the map and choose "To here".
2. Tap "Directions". The panel shows "Directions to" and the place.
3. Choose how you travel: "Drive", "Walk", "Bike" or "Off-road".
4. Cache shows "Finding the way", then the route with its distance and time.
5. Tap "Start" to begin turn-by-turn guidance.

Directions start from where you are, so Cache needs your location. If it says "Your position is not known yet. Turn on location to get directions from where you are.", tap the locate button ("Find me") and allow location.

Road directions work offline when the region is downloaded. Without it, they need a connection.

If part of a route has no road, the panel says so and shows that part dashed.

If no route is found, the panel says why, for example "No way found between these places" or "One of these places is outside the downloaded maps". Try another way of travel, or download the region.

## Off-road directions

"Off-road" in the directions panel plans a route away from roads.

1. In "Maps", open a place's card and tap "Directions".
2. Tap "Off-road".
3. Choose "On foot" or "Vehicle".
4. Choose land access:
   - "Stay on open land": keeps to land that is open to the public.
   - "Usual": the normal choice.
   - "Emergency: any land": ignores land rules. Cache warns: "Ignores land rules: the route may cross private or closed land." Use it only in an emergency.
5. Changing a choice plans the route again. Tap "Start" to go.

Off-road directions use the off-road data that comes with every region download. If you see "Off-road needs off-road data for this area", download the region for this area, or choose "Walk" or "Drive" instead.

These choices are not kept: after Cache restarts, directions start as "Drive", and off-road starts as "On foot" with "Usual".

## Follow a route

After you tap "Start" in the directions panel, Cache guides you turn by turn.

- The banner at the top shows the next turn. At the start it may say "Getting your position" until the first GPS fix.
- If you leave the route, it says "Rerouting" and works out a new way from where you are.
- Near the end it says "Arriving", then "You have arrived".
- Spoken directions are on at the start. Tap "Turn the voice off" on the guidance bar to silence them, and "Turn the voice on" to bring them back.
- Tap "End" to stop guiding.

Guidance keeps running with the screen locked. On Android a notification titled "Cache navigation" shows while it runs.

Cache asks to send notifications when guidance starts. Guidance still works if you refuse.

To open a route the assistant planned, tap "Open navigation" under its answer in "Ask". If you are already guiding, Cache says "Finish or end the route first".

## Map layers and offline maps

The "Layers" button on the right of "Maps" opens the layers sheet.

- Each layer has a switch. Its line says whether it is "Downloaded for offline", "Streams when online" or "Needs a connection".
- A layer laid over the map has an "Opacity" slider when it is on.
- "Show my position" turns your position on the map on or off.
- "Download a region" opens "Regions".
- "Get more layers" opens the list of every layer.

Layers stream when you are online and keep the parts you viewed. To keep a whole area for offline use, download its region: every region includes routing, map, places and off-road. See the section Download a region in chapter 1. There is no separate download for one layer's area.

Some layers cannot be kept offline: "This layer's terms do not allow keeping it offline."

## Library and reader

"Library" holds everything you keep for reading offline in one list: reference works from Kiwix (ZIM files) and your own libraries. Each row has an icon, a name, a short description and its size. A badge shows only when something needs attention, for example "Update ready".

- Tap a row to open it full screen.
- Press and hold a row, or tap "..." in the opened item, for a menu: "Check for update", "Change icon", "Add to Home" and "Delete".
- "Add to Library" adds more. See the section Add to your Library in chapter 1.

The icon starts as the website icon of the source. "Change icon" lets you pick a picture from the phone.

"Delete" shows what it frees and asks you to confirm. You can download it again later.

In the reader:
- "Search this ZIM" searches inside the open work.
- "Find in page" finds words on the page, with "Previous" and "Next".
- "Back", "Forward" and "Main page" are at the bottom.
- "More" > "Add to Home" puts the page on "Home".

Messages you may see:
- "This ZIM is still being indexed. Text search works when that is done." Wait for indexing to finish; you can still read pages.
- "That link is on the web. It is not available offline." The page links outside the work.

The assistant searches your libraries and cites them in "Ask".

## Videos

"Videos" lets you watch and save videos from PeerTube sites. Open it from "Modules" (or pin it to the bar).

To add a site:
1. Tap "Add an instance".
2. Type the site's address, for example tube.example.org.
3. Tap "Add".

To watch:
1. Tap the instance. Choose "Latest" or "Channels", or search it.
2. Tap a video, then "Play".

In the player:
- Tap the speed button (it shows 1x) to pick a playback speed from 0.5x to 2x. "Normal" is 1x.
- Tap "Full screen" for true full screen: the video alone, turned to landscape for a wide video, with the system bars hidden. Tap the screen to show the controls. Back, or "Leave full screen", leaves it.

To save a video for offline:
1. On the video, tap "Download".
2. Pick a size. Smaller sizes save space on the phone.
3. When it is saved, the video says "Saved on this phone" and "Play saved copy" plays it without a connection.

Saved videos are under "Saved on this phone" at the top of "Videos". A stopped download can be continued with "Continue". To remove saved videos, delete them here in "Videos", or go to "Settings" > "Storage" > "Videos and weather".

To log in (only needed for videos the site keeps behind a login):
1. Open the instance and tap its account button ("Account and details").
2. Under "Log in (optional)", enter "User name" and "Password", and the "Two-factor code" if asked.
3. Tap "Log in".

The same sheet has "Log out" and "Remove this instance".

## Weather

Open "Weather" from "Modules", or tap the "Weather and alerts" card on "Home".

- The box at the top says when the data was last updated, and why it may be old. Tap "Update now" to fetch it again, or pull the screen down.
- Forecast cards show the weather for the watched place.
- "Happening nearby" lists alerts, fires and the other sources you switched on, each with its age.
- If you watch more than one place, tap a place's chip at the top to switch.

Every alert is listed here, whatever you chose under "Pop up for" in "Notifications".

Tap any item for its details. "Ask about this" starts a new chat in "Ask" asking what it means for you and what you should do.

If an alert notification opens Cache, it goes straight to that alert. If it says "That alert has ended or is no longer in the data.", the alert is over or has dropped out of the feed.

If "Weather" says "Weather is off." or "No place is being watched.", tap "Turn on weather", or "Choose a place instead".

The tune button at the top ("Weather and alerts settings") opens the settings for sources.

Weather needs a connection to update. Offline, Cache shows the last data and how old it is.
