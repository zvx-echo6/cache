# What's new

The lines the "What's new in 1.0.N" sheet shows once after an update: one "## <version>" heading, then one "- " line each,
written by the owner (lead words in **bold**). A version with no lines shows no sheet; the app never writes them itself.

## 1.1.0
- **A new Library:** four places in one: ZIM Collections, Bookshelves (your books on the numbered shelves of the Cache Decimal System, each with its own call number), Catalog (every search index, with Pause and Resume) and Cache Documentation (this guide, What's new and the credits).
- **Add your own source:** plug in any web service that gives data, pick what to show, and it works like the other weather and alerts sources.
- **Share just what you choose:** each kind under Sharing and sync opens a list of its items, each with its own switch and Sync now, and each linked device has its own page with Sync now, Rename and Unlink. The Shared folder is gone; its files stay where they are.
- **Settings across your devices:** each linked device's page has Settings: Match, Send mine or Off. Choose what goes group by group under What's included; API keys go only when you tick them, encrypted for that device.

## 1.0.30
- **Search inside your ZIMs:** PDFs, e-books and video captions inside your libraries are now searchable, and answers cite the exact page, chapter or moment.
- **Built quietly:** Cache makes them searchable on its own in the background, going easy on battery and heat. Settings shows the progress, with a speed choice under Advanced.
- **Shared between devices:** search and map indexes built on one linked device are used on your others, so each is built only once.
- **PDF viewer:** read PDFs right in Cache, find text, jump to a page, and turn on dark pages.
- **Videos, redesigned:** a new player with captions, quality choice, resume where you stopped, and a transcript beside the video on larger screens.
- **Search your videos:** find saved videos by what's said in them.
- **Video sites:** sign in with whatever methods your site offers, and play private videos.
- **No model needed:** Cache works for search and reading without a model; saved chats stay readable, and Ask returns when you add one.
- **Import notes by tag:** tag a note #cachesync in Obsidian or Logseq to bring it into Cache.
- **Wide screens:** a layout made for computers and the big iPad sideways.
- **Fixes:** the Kiwix icon shows correctly; Regions no longer syncs files each device builds for itself; Add to Library no longer appears twice on wide screens.

## 1.0.29
- **Emergency alerts:** official IPAWS alerts for your area, along with the weather alerts.
- **Faster downloads:** region maps download many times faster, with speed and time left shown.
- **Downloads:** every download shows what it is doing, with speed and time left, and can be paused and resumed.
- **Models:** Gemma 4 E2B tuned for Cache is now recommended; uncensored models are listed apart, under Experimental.
- **Faster places:** places and addresses are ready in seconds after a download, not minutes.
- **Read about it:** places with a Wikipedia article show a short summary, and open the article.
- **Repeaters:** tap one on the map for its details; each repeater list is now a notebook.
- **Weather and alerts settings:** choose which places weather watches: your location, regions and saved places.
- **iPad and tablets:** two panes side by side, and every list can be hidden for more room.
- **Update notices:** Cache tells you when a new version is ready.
- **Fixes:** leaving a library always works; pictures in some libraries no longer show blank on iPhone; the Library list updates right after a sync; stuck downloads can be carried on; Obsidian and Logseq only sync Cache's own notes.
